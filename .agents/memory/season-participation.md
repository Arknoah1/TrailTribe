---
name: Season participation
description: The durable distinction between rider accounts, pods, and current-season participation.
---

Current-season rider participation is separate from account activation, approval, household membership, and pod assignment. A rider taking a season off must retain their account and history and must not be placed in a synthetic “Not Active” pod.

**Why:** Pods drive coaching groups and event audiences, while account activation controls access. Reusing either would create unintended permission, messaging, and historical effects and would not support mixed households cleanly.

**How to apply:** Current-season roster counts, ordinary rider audiences, and reminders should default to active riders. Administrative all-rider views must still reveal season-off and undecided riders and allow reactivation.
