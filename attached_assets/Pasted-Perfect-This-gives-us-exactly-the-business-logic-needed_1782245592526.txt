## Project Management Plan: MTBChat Volunteer Module

### Feature Overview

A dynamic, event-driven volunteer scheduling system integrated into the existing mountain bike team communication application. It replaces static spreadsheet tracking with user profile integration, multi-event registration flows, and admin-managed task templating.

### Core Architecture & Logic Flow

1. **The Volunteer Sign-Up Funnel:**
* **Step 1 (Availability):** User is presented with the full season calendar and checks off which race weekends they are attending.
* **Step 2 (Task Assignment):** Based on their active weekends, they are shown a structured grid or list of open tasks. They can use a "Select All" toggle to claim a recurring task (e.g., *Food Prep*) across all their active weekends, or pick distinct tasks dynamically per event.




2. **Database Relationships:**
* `Events` have many `Tasks`.
* `Tasks` have a `max_volunteers` constraint, a `description`, and belong to a specific category (e.g., Coaching, Bike Village).


* `VolunteerSlots` join a `User`, a `Task`, and an `Event` together.


3. **Admin Controls:** Admins manage a **Master Template** of tasks. When creating a new race weekend, they can clone the template, which instantiates the default tasks and volunteer caps for that event, with the ability to add, edit, or delete items on a one-off basis (like the *Winthrop Camp Check-In*).
